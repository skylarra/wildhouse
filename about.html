There are two things I have always wanted to be: an artist and a wildlife conservationist. Wildhouse Lane is my way of living both dreams in hopes people will come along for the ride.

Every piece is designed and finished by hand in South Carolina. 10% of all profits are donated to wildlife conservation efforts, and I share how much is donated along the way.

What we make

Suncatchers, keychains, stickers, magnets, patches, and tees — all inspired by spooky nature art and the creatures we share the woods with.
