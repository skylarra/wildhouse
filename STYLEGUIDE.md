## Brand Personality

Curious

Handcrafted

Storybook

---

# Visual Style

Large photography

Generous spacing

Elegant typography

Minimal clutter

Organic layouts

Natural textures

Subtle movement

---

# Colors

Use earthy neutrals.

Deep greens.

Warm creams.

Walnut browns.

Soft charcoal.

Muted gold accents.

Avoid bright saturated colors.

---

# Typography

Readable.

Elegant.

Not overly decorative.

Use whitespace generously.

---

# Components

Cards

Rounded corners only when intentional.

Natural shadows.

Never excessive.

Buttons

Simple.

Comfortable padding.

Gentle hover animation.

Navigation

Minimal.

Easy to understand.

Mobile first.

---

# Motion

Animations should feel calm.

200–400ms.

Fade.

Slide.

Scale subtly.

Avoid flashy effects.

---

# Photography

Large.

Natural lighting.

Real textures.

Wood.

Paper.

Plants.

Stone.

Never use stock photography unless temporary.

---

# User Experience

Every page should answer:

Where am I?

What can I do?

What should I look at next?

Reduce friction wherever possible.
